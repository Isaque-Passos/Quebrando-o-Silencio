# Prompts e origem das imagens

Método: geração integrada de imagens (image_gen), em 02/10/2026. Os arquivos finais foram copiados para design/assets. Nenhuma imagem do jogo de referência foi usada como arquivo de entrada ou incorporada à entrega.

Referências consultadas para entender o formato: [Amor Doce](https://www.amordoce.com/) e [captura publicada na página do aplicativo](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?hl=pt-BR&id=beemoov.amoursucre.android). As imagens entregues são novas gerações. A proximidade pretendida é a linguagem de visual novel, com cenário, retrato e escolhas, não reprodução dos elementos do jogo.

## cenario-quarto.png

Use case: illustration-story. Create a finished production background for an original Brazilian educational visual novel for children, chapter 1, morning at home. ONE image only, landscape 16:9, ideally 1536x864 or 1920x1080. Scene: welcoming modest Brazilian student's bedroom, late childhood, gender neutral, eye-level wide view. Bed with sage-green cover on the left, a light wood study desk and books beneath a window, blue school backpack beside desk, warm cream walls, muted blue curtain, small corkboard with unlettered paper shapes, simple tiled or wooden floor. Door on right opens inward. Foreground center-right has clear standing space for a separately layered adult character; lower 28% remains visually quiet because a game dialogue panel will cover it. Camera comfortable interior perspective, enough view of room, no distortion. Art direction: polished hand-drawn 2D anime-inspired visual-novel background, fine controlled brown linework, soft cel-shaded painted coloring, gentle sunlight, believable everyday architecture, age-appropriate welcoming tone. Palette warm ivory #FFF8ED, ink navy #25344A, dusty blue #345C8C, sage #D9E8D5, small honey yellow accents #F2C66D; natural colors not monochrome. Visually rich but uncluttered. NO characters, people, text, lettering, logo, UI, dialogue bubbles, frames or watermark. Entire canvas is the environment, no border. Original room design, do not reproduce any existing game environment.
